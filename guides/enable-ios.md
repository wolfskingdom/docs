# The Wolf’s Kingdom · iOS Enable Guide ✅

> ⚠️ **Why this is needed**
>
> Discord flagged our server as **Age-Restricted**. Until you flip one setting, you might not see **The Kingdom** on iOS.
>
> ***THIS BULLSHIT IS ENTIRELY FIXABLE*** 💪

---

## TL;DR (1-minute fix)

- **On iOS (Safari/Chrome)**: open **discord.com** → <kbd>☰</kbd> → <kbd>⚙︎ User Settings</kbd> → **Content & Social** → toggle **Allow access to age-restricted servers on iOS** ✅  
- **On Desktop**: <kbd>⚙︎ User Settings</kbd> → **Content & Social** → toggle **Allow access to age-restricted servers on iOS** ✅

---

## Mobile-Only Users

> ℹ️ You **can’t** change this setting inside the iOS app itself — you must use a **mobile web browser**.

1. **GO TO** **[discord.com](https://discord.com/)** in your browser.  
2. At the top, **TAP** **Open Discord** to launch the web app.  
3. **TAP** the **hamburger menu** <kbd>☰</kbd> (top-left).  
   - You’ll see your **User Panel** with a **gear icon**.  
4. **TAP** <kbd>⚙︎</kbd> **User Settings**.  
5. **TAP** **Content & Social**.  
6. **ENABLE**: **“Allow access to age-restricted servers on iOS.”**  
   - Toggle **ON** and you’re done.  
   - Open the **Discord iOS app** — **The Kingdom** should now be visible.

> ✅ If you still don’t see the server, force-quit the Discord app and relaunch it.

---

## Desktop Users (macOS/Windows/Linux)

1. In Discord, **CLICK** <kbd>⚙︎</kbd> **User Settings** (bottom-left).  
2. **CLICK** **Content & Social**.  
3. **ENABLE**: **“Allow access to age-restricted servers on iOS.”**  
   - Toggle **ON** and you’re done.  
   - This updates your account and fixes iOS access.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Setting missing?** Update to the **latest** Discord app and try again via **discord.com** in a browser.  
- **Still blocked on iOS?**  
  - Force-quit and relaunch the Discord app.  
  - Log out and back in.  
  - Re-check the toggle under **Content & Social**.  
- **Multiple accounts?** Make sure you toggled it on the **same account** you use for **The Kingdom**.
